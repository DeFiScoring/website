How the score is built

Five pillars, weighted as shown above: loan reliability (35%), portfolio health (25%), liquidity provision (15%), account age (15%) and governance (10%). Loan reliability reads Aave V3, Spark, Compound V3 and Morpho Blue; liquidity provision values live Uniswap V3 positions in USD; account age takes the oldest first transaction across every scored chain. Scored on {{ site.defi.scored_chains | join: ", " }}. A pillar whose source could not be read is held at a neutral score and marked estimated — it is never presented as an observed result.
